What HG Intel does, in plain terms.

  • Continuous source monitoring

    A curated registry of Australian regulators, standards bodies, peak bodies and the evidence base, checked on a regular schedule rather than when someone remembers.

  • Change detection

    Changes are picked up from the source itself, so a quiet edit to a page nobody announced is still caught.

  • Registered Nurse review

    Every source passes a Registered Nurse checkpoint before anything from it reaches a customer. AI-assisted content is reviewed by a human before it publishes, and it is labelled.

  • Classified for your setting

    Sources are tagged by the care settings and areas of practice they apply to, so a hospital group and a home care provider do not receive the same undifferentiated list.

  • Mapped to your standards

    Change is mapped to the standards and obligations your organisation is actually assessed against, rather than left as a pile of links to interpret.

  • Linked to its source

    Every item links to its source document, so you can verify rather than trust.

  • A record you can use

    Acknowledgements and an evidence trail your organisation can take into an assessment or straight into a board paper.

What we watch: our source coverage.

The registry itself is the work of years, so we do not publish it. What we will say is what kind of source goes in it, and which areas of practice it covers.

Regulators and government

Commonwealth and state regulators, departments and commissions whose decisions create obligations for providers.

Standards and accreditation

The standards bodies and accreditation schemes your organisation is actually assessed against, and the guidance issued alongside them.

Peak bodies

Peak bodies and safety organisations, whose position statements shape what good practice is taken to mean.

The evidence base

Clinical guidelines, coronial and inquiry findings, and the research that moves practice ahead of the rules catching up.

Areas of practice covered

Wound care Medication safety Falls Infection prevention Dementia care Palliative care Nutrition Continence Nursing practice Hospital Aged care Community and home care Respite NDIS and disability Medical devices Work health and safety AI and technology and more

Areas of practice are the content taxonomy inside the product. They are not the same thing as the sectors listed above, which describe who HG Intel is built for. The registry keeps growing, and every addition passes the same Registered Nurse checkpoint as the ones already in it.

Where your data sits, and what we will not do with it.

Australian hosted

The application and its database are hosted in Australia, in Google Cloud's Sydney region. Supporting services are disclosed in the privacy notice.

Not training material

Your mappings and your usage are yours. We do not train models on them and we do not sell them. They are shared only with the service providers listed in our privacy policy.

Reviewed, cited, and not advice

Every source passes a Registered Nurse checkpoint, every item links to its source, and HG Intel is decision support. A qualified human decides.
