We are heading to Digital Health Festival 2027

8 October 2026

By Naomi AlefelderFounder, HG Intel

19 & 20 May 2027 · Melbourne

Meet us at DHF27

Come and find us
Intel by Holistic Governance Start-up pod S18

HG Intel will be at Digital Health Festival 2027 in Melbourne next May, exhibiting at start-up pod S18.

DHF27 runs on Wednesday 19 and Thursday 20 May 2027 in Bays 8 to 18 at the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre (MCEC). It is two days where the people shaping Australian digital health are in one place: clinicians, founders, health system leaders, and the people actually buying and running the technology the sector depends on. For a product built for Australian health and care providers, that is the room to be in.

We will be showing what we have been building. HG Intel tracks regulatory and best practice change across a curated registry of Australian sources, puts every source through a Registered Nurse review checkpoint, and maps what changed to the standards you are assessed against. Where we are up to, stated plainly: the intelligence pipeline runs today, the provider-facing application is in build, and we open to a small group of providers before general release.

We will also be showing what comes out of our founding design partner program. In early 2027 a small founding cohort of provider organisations will be putting HG Intel to work inside their own governance, quality and board reporting, and telling us plainly what earns its place and what does not. They decide what gets built and what gets cut. What we show in May will be whatever that cohort tells us is worth showing.

We exist to prevent that heart-sinking feeling of realising you have missed or misinterpreted a requirement. Come and say hi. If you would like to book a demo or catch up at the festival, email our founder Naomi Alefelder at info@hg-intel.com.au.
