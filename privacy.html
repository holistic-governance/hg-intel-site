2. Google user data

If HG Intel lets you sign in with your Google account, we receive only the basic profile information Google shares with your consent: your name, email address, Google account identifier and, where available, your profile image. We use this information only to:

  • authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • create and maintain your HG Intel account and display your name in the service
  • contact you about your account and the service

We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google data, and we do not sell, rent or share Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising or to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Google user data is not read by people except where you ask us to (for example, for support), where it is needed for security, or where the law requires it.

HG Intel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove HG Intel's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and ask us to delete your account (see section 8).

4. AI and decisions made by computer programs

In short: HG Intel does not use AI or automated rules to make significant decisions about people. AI helps find and summarise regulatory changes, your team stays in control of what happens next, and your name, email address and role are never sent to the AI.

We align with the Privacy Act's transparency requirements on automated decisions. These ask organisations to explain when they use computer programs, including AI, with personal information to make or help make decisions that could significantly affect people. Here is how that applies to HG Intel.

No significant decisions about you

HG Intel does not use computer programs or AI to make, or help make, decisions that could significantly affect your rights or interests. It does not score, rank or profile people, and it does not use health or other sensitive information about you to make decisions.

Everyday automatic rules

Like most online services, HG Intel uses a few simple, pre-set rules to run the service. They use limited personal information and apply automatically, without a person checking each one:

How HG Intel uses AI

HG Intel uses AI (Anthropic's Claude, see section 6) to help find, summarise and classify regulatory and industry changes, and map them to standards. The AI works on public regulatory content and on content you choose to submit, such as a newsletter you forward to HG Intel. Its output supports your team's review; it does not make decisions. We do not send your name, email address or role to the AI, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

HG Intel is built for regulatory content, not patient, resident or staff records. As an extra safeguard, it checks text you submit for care details about a named person or health identifiers, and removes them or holds the item back before anything reaches the AI. Please keep personal and health information about patients, residents, clients and staff out of content you submit.

For organisations using HG Intel

HG Intel supports governance decisions about regulatory change, not decisions about people. Acknowledgement records show who reviewed an update and when, so your organisation can evidence its governance process. If your organisation chooses to use HG Intel information in decisions about individuals, such as staff, your own privacy policy may need to mention it. We are happy to provide plain-language information about how HG Intel works to help: email info@hg-intel.com.au.

7. Retention

We keep account information for as long as your account is active, and delete it within 90 days after the account is closed unless we must keep it longer by law. We keep interest registrations in Holistic Governance's internal records system on Google Cloud (Sydney) for up to 3 years after we last contact you, unless you become a customer or ask us to delete them sooner. To stop hearing from us, email info@hg-intel.com.au and we will stop emailing you; we keep a record that you opted out so you are not added again by mistake, and we will delete that too if you ask. We keep enquiry emails for up to 12 months after we last correspond with you. Server logs are kept for a short operational period and then discarded. Copies may remain in our secure backups, which are used only to restore our systems; if a backup is ever restored, we delete that information again.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@hg-intel.com.au. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

9. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will contain it, assess it promptly, and notify affected individuals and the OAIC as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
