2. Google user data

If HG Intel lets you sign in with your Google account, we receive only the basic profile information Google shares with your consent: your name, email address, Google account identifier and, where available, your profile image. We use this information only to:

  • authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • create and maintain your HG Intel account and display your name in the service
  • contact you about your account and the service

We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google data, and we do not sell, rent or share Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising or to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Google user data is not read by people except where you ask us to (for example, for support), where it is needed for security, or where the law requires it.

HG Intel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove HG Intel's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and ask us to delete your account (see section 7).

3. How we use your information

  • to provide, operate and support HG Intel
  • to respond to your enquiries
  • to tell you about changes to the service or these policies
  • to keep the service secure and meet our legal obligations

6. Retention

We keep account information for as long as your account is active, and delete it within 90 days after the account is closed unless we must keep it longer by law. We keep interest registrations until you ask us to remove you, or until two years after HG Intel's general release, whichever comes first. We keep enquiry emails for up to 12 months after we last correspond with you. Server logs are kept for a short operational period and then discarded.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@hg-intel.com.au. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

8. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will contain it, assess it promptly, and notify affected individuals and the OAIC as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
