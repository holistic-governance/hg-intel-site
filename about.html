Built by a Registered Nurse who has run clinical governance, and now builds the software for it.

Naomi Alefelder

Founder & Technical Lead · Registered Nurse · Board Director

Naomi is the founder of HG Intel by Holistic Governance. She has spent more than a decade running clinical governance, accreditation and risk across hospital, aged care, disability and community health, and now builds the software she needed in those roles.

She leads HG Intel’s product and technical architecture, supported by software developers. That covers the data model and PostgreSQL design on Google Cloud, the TypeScript codebase and CI pipeline, test‑driven development, and the architecture decision records behind every significant design choice. She owns the source registry, and is the Registered Nurse behind the curation checkpoint that every source in HG Intel has to pass.

ASD Cyber Security Partner

Holistic Governance is a Business Partner in the Australian Signals Directorate's (ASD) Cyber Security Partnership Program. Through this program we receive alerts, advisories, and guidance from the Australian Cyber Security Centre, helping keep our understanding of the national cyber threat landscape current and informing the data assurance and clinical governance work we do for the providers and health technology organisations we support.

Learn more about the program at cyber.gov.au.
